How to read this

A pattern, not a proof of causation

The sample shows recurring nominal-to-verbal shifts, but subtitle translation also reflects timing, dialogue rhythm, and the choices of unknown translators. The site treats category labels as an interpretable first pass and keeps verification status visible.
