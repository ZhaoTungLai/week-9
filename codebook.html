Classification

Codebook

Five categories for classifying the semantic role of an English nominalization and its Chinese verbal rendering.

Each entry receives one category based on the English nominal selected by NV Atlas. The category describes the main kind of process named by the nominal, not the grammatical form of the Chinese sentence. Rules are applied in the order shown. A term that meets more than one rule goes to the earliest category.

01

Institutional procedure

Definition. A nominalization that names an action regulated by an institution, legal process, public office, or formal organization.

Theory source. Institutional discourse and genre analysis; the category draws on the idea that recurring institutional genres stabilize particular action nouns.

Decision rule. Choose this when the nominal denotes appointment, authorization, punishment, legal process, employment status, or a formal transaction, even if it also refers to speech or affect.

Positivepermission → 允许
Negativeadmiration → 欣赏

Boundary case. An examination can be a speech event, but if the context is a formal medical or legal procedure, it belongs here; a classroom discussion remains speech/cognition.

02

Speech & cognition

Definition. A nominalization that names communication, interpretation, thought, memory, perception, or a mental decision.

Theory source. Cognitive semantics and pragmatics; meaning is treated as a construal of an event rather than a fixed word-to-word mapping.

Decision rule. Choose this when the English term primarily concerns saying, asking, understanding, remembering, judging, or choosing. It must not have priority as an institutional procedure.

Positiveexplanation → 解释
Negativepayment → 付钱

Boundary case. Confession can be religious speech or a police procedure; the broad label remains speech/cognition unless the entry explicitly refers to a formal legal charge.

03

Affect & embodiment

Definition. A nominalization that names an emotion, bodily process, health event, or physical experience of a person.

Theory source. Embodiment and experiential semantics; emotions and bodily events are treated as processes experienced by a participant.

Decision rule. Choose this for feelings and bodily/medical processes such as dance, cure, infection, or disappointment, unless an institutional rule clearly applies.

Positivedance → 跳舞
Negativeelection → 选举

Boundary case. Treatment can be a medical process or an interpersonal action. The medical sense goes here; repeated mistreatment is material action.

04

Social relation

Definition. A nominalization that names a state, role, connection, position, or relation between people, institutions, or things.

Theory source. Systemic functional linguistics; relational processes connect participants rather than primarily describing an action or mental state.

Decision rule. Choose this for presence, protection, assistance, possession, position, membership, and similar terms. It applies after the higher-priority institutional and speech rules.

Positiveprotection → 保护
Negativedestruction → 毁灭

Boundary case. Assistance can be an action, but its main function is a relation of support between participants; it stays social relation.

05

Material action

Definition. A nominalization that names a concrete event, physical action, movement, change, or use of force.

Theory source. Systemic functional linguistics; material processes are doings and happenings in the physical world.

Decision rule. Choose this as the residual category when the nominal describes a concrete action or event and does not meet the institutional, speech, affect, or relation rules.

Positivedestruction → 毁灭
Negativepresence → 出现

Boundary case. Invasion is a military action; it is placed here unless the entry explicitly names a political or legal proceeding.

Application order

  1. Institutional procedure
  2. Speech & cognition
  3. Affect & embodiment
  4. Social relation
  5. Material action

When a term could fit two categories, use the earliest matching category. Every category assignment is recorded in data/corpus.csv, and the full build rules are in scripts/build_corpus.py.
